## Constraints and design

The plan satisfies the governing one-home rule: "Call the home; never write a second body." Import already replays three physical streams; iterative TermValue folds, CDT rewriting and destination namespace selection are reused. It avoids a second classifier/freezer/parser, semantic features, new runtime dependencies, external submissions and protected sibling changes. Public DatasetMut signatures and Implicit default remain intact during additive qualification. Shared RDF1.2 identity, portability and deterministic output requirements are explicit.

The proposed role-qualified membership fixes the owning defect rather than patching Update's wrapper. Current base_occurrences combines three physical streams beneath one QuadKey; suppression/restoration therefore needs kind-qualified keys. The selective restoration example explicitly keeps the other role suppressed and computes physical graph/cardinality changes once. Ordinary value mutation retains whole-value restoration/removal. Moving automatic classification into the owner, with typed publication afterward, prevents lazy view recategorization from contradicting accounting. Indexed affected-subject/graph normalization avoids an unnecessary full-base rewrite.

LOAD's fresh per-successful-resolution map uses original(label,scope), shared across nested terms and genuine CDT references. Request-wide counters and destination-owned unused/suppressed/CDT identities prevent collisions across requests and mixed mint operations. Resolver/admission/cancellation/SILENT ordering, empty remembered destination and physical charging are preserved. Owned source snapshots protect COPY/MOVE destination clearing; transferred blanks retain their identities. Preparation before row publication does not silently promise a new transaction mechanism.
