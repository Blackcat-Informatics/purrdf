The SHACL RL lowerer has two real semantics encoded through fourth-slot sentinels. `WHERE DATA`/`NOT DATA` body atoms address an immutable base-graph mirror (`BASE_GRAPH`) while ordinary atoms read the growing evaluation graph; deleting that distinction would let inferred facts satisfy base-only patterns. An empty rule head currently derives an unobservable `EMPTY_HEAD` marker because the generic clause IR interprets an empty head as inconsistency; deleting that marker without a replacement changes a legal no-output rule into a refusal or a visible fact. Both must be resolved before arity-three RL is claimed.

For empty-head RL rules, add a distinct no-output head disposition at the existing clause/schedule/emit home: evaluate body and guards under the same scheduling/governor/first-error law, then emit no fact/derivation. Do not reuse the existing inconsistency head form, and do not insert a marker triple. This is a source-backed design direction, not implemented or qualified code. Preserve the quad rule API/bytes and validate base-only, negated-base, empty-head, recursive, variable-predicate and guarded cases against independent expected models and the all-default quad oracle.
