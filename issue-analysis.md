The witness-question distinction is necessary and sound: support asks whether a
supported witness exists; contradiction asks whether both polarities exist;
closed absence asks for a complete negative search. A first positive witness
cannot certify the absence of a later opposed witness. The plan explicitly
separates those questions at query specification and does not promise a full
four-valued classification from a first-support stop. Standard result carriers
and ordinary default query bag semantics remain compatible; richer projections
are supplied through the same execution, not a replacement evaluator.
