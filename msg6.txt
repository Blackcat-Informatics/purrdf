Carry the canonical switch through `purrdf-rdf` and the PACK container

`purrdf-rdf` re-exports the new canonical names and the `rdfc12` module.
`canonical_flat_nquads` returns the canonical document of the labelling, and
`canonical_flat_nquads_with` is the RDFC-1.0 entry by name. The flat-view
benches and the tests of the RDFC-1.0 flat presentation call it through
`rdfc12`. The streamed-digest test is removed because the digest is now of the
line sequence, not of the document bytes.

The PACK container writes format version 2, whose header holds the digest of
the canonical line sequence, and still reads version 1. `verify_pack`
recomputes a version-1 digest with the RDFC-1.0 function that recorded it and a
version-2 digest with the labelling, and `PackView::format_version` reports
which. The earlier writer's bytes stay pinned through the version-1 reading of
the new frame, and a new golden fixes the version-2 bytes.

GTS compaction certificates and the refold digest stay on RDFC-1.0, because
the frozen GTS vectors are compared byte for byte and the wire format is
governed in `gmeow-gts`.
