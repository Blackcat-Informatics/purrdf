feat(shapes): SHACL dated profile selection and complete validation reports

Closes #402

## What changed

- **Dated SHACL profiles.** `purrdf_shapes::ShaclProfile` offers `LEGACY`
  (the default, unchanged), `REC_20170720` and `WD_20260918`. Any other
  identifier is refused as `UnsupportedProfile`. Pre-binding admission
  (VALUES, MINUS, SERVICE, AS, hidden subquery projection) follows the
  selected law and refuses with a typed `AdmissionRefusal`. Dated admission
  reads the query as written, keeping the algebra as parsed when the
  pre-binding rewrite changes it.
- **Complete validation reports.** `CompleteValidationReport` and
  `CompleteReportGraph` sit beside the unchanged legacy report types. They
  carry `sh:sourceConstraint`, recursive `sh:detail` with its own evidence,
  the dated message order, and blank-node correspondence back to the data or
  shapes acquisition. `purrdf_validate` adds `validate_complete_*`,
  `complete_report_payload/_to_json/_to_sarif_string` and
  `complete_validation_status`.
- **Community SHACL corpus** (`corpora/community/`, 64 cases) and the
  unpublished `conformance-kit` crate. Every complete report is graded by
  graph isomorphism in source context. The runner covers the fresh,
  prepared and restored-product routes.

## Evidence

- Community corpus: the SHACL 2017-07-20 profile passes 57 of 57 and the
  SHACL 1.2 2026-09-18 profile passes 58 of 58. In total that is 115
  executions and 535 observations, all passing.
- The existing SHACL conformance lanes are unchanged. Hosted CI is green
  on the exact head, and `.deficiencies` is empty.
