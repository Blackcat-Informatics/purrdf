# Original-coordinate preparation and identified source atlas

Source-linear edges interpolate the exact original longitude and latitude in
their explicitly registered axis order. A written 170 to −170 degree edge has
longitude change −340 degrees. Pole and cut identification never changes this
interpolation. Z and M remain the original carrier metadata and supply no height
or surface metric. Every coordinate carries its reference binding; preparation,
membership and cover operations refuse a different binding.

A written polygon uses its closed chart exterior with holes removed. The caller
can explicitly select its closed complement. Ring order and orientation remain
source data, independent of this selected side. Empty line inputs produce no
areal interior. Empty and whole surface are explicit region values. A collection
inventory keeps curves and areal rings separate, enabling dimension totals
without counting polygon boundaries as both curve and area components.

Inside a chart the existing exact segment and crossing-number predicates own
membership. Longitude −180 and +180 identify the same physical meridian. Every
longitude at latitude ±90 identifies one pole. At an incident chart edge, the
atlas builds the finite arrangement of exact incident directions together with
the four coordinate axes. Each adjacent sector is strictly smaller than π, so
the positive sum of its two bounding rays lies strictly inside that sector.
Exact ray/segment intersections and the nearest pole bound a positive interval
before any further event. Its half interval supplies a witness whose location
is constant throughout the sector. The union is interior when every sector is
inside and boundary when inside and outside sectors meet. This removes internal
union edges and chart cuts without a sampling tolerance.

At a pole, half the smallest positive vertex latitude separation defines an
annulus containing no non-polar vertex or horizontal edge. Exact intersections
of all source edges with its latitude circle partition all longitude sectors.
Their exact midpoint witnesses classify the complete sectors. All sectors inside
means pole interior; mixed sectors mean boundary; all exterior sectors mean
exterior unless a written edge collapses to the pole itself. This distinguishes a
complete polar cap from a wedge while identifying every input pole longitude.

The area gauge is −∮Q(φ)dλ with Q(0)=0, increasing longitude east, and cut
longitude in [−180,180). Exact pole frames use longitude zero. A selected
north-to-south geodesic with forward azimuth zero continues on meridian −180;
its zero-length pole cuts are north 0→−180 and south −180→0. Their signed area
is +2πQ(90), whereas south-to-north forward zero continues on meridian zero and
has zero area. Normalized azimuth differences do not replace these branch cuts.

Azimuth/length arcs retain their exact original start, azimuth and metre length
as mathematical inputs. Their correctly quantized direct endpoint is separate
materialization evidence. It never becomes an exact mathematical ring closure.
Endpoint-only shortest arcs retain both exact original endpoints. Their inverse
certificate proves one shortest branch before preparation succeeds. Distinct
equal branches and a continuum of pole-to-pole branches are ambiguity errors;
unresolved multiplicity is a precision error. Rounded inverse azimuths and
lengths never redefine the endpoint-selected mathematical arc. Equivalent
invocation proofs and admitted policies do not change arc equality.

The complement of a polygon union is represented as the complement of that
complete union. It does not distribute as the union of component complements.
The atlas classifies the incident sectors before selecting their complementary
side, so internal union edges and empty complementary cut edges do not become
physical boundaries.

Prepared source-region covers reuse the complete cell footprint cap. Latitude
is bounded by its angular radius; away from poles, longitude is bounded using
cos(φ)≥1−|φ|/90 over the complete latitude band. A cap crossing a pole has all
longitudes. These rectangles enclose the entire assigned footprint, including
the independently proved Q62 assignment and fixed-axis quantization guards.
Exact source-segment/rectangle intersections, including tangencies and translated
cut aliases, detect possible boundary contact. If every boundary is separated,
the connected physical footprint has constant region membership, established by
the shared atlas at its axis. Otherwise the cell is geometrically straddling and
strict traversal refines it to the requested maximum. Empty and whole surface
classify directly. Arithmetic uncertainty refuses or refines; it cannot select a
coarser successful cover. This classifier has a distinct cover law identity and
does not alter point keys or the existing disk/box cover law.

The shared original-carrier inventory walks the complete tree before preparing
or materializing another coordinate graph. It records actual owned vector
capacities and rational limb allocations, the complete vertex count, iterative
nesting depth, charged work and conservative simultaneous workspace. It applies
no geographic range or axis interpretation. The same walk governs geographic
preparation and coordinate-operation images; the geometric subdivision limit is
separate from source-carrier nesting. Original spare capacity is included even
when a carrier has very few populated coordinates.

Written coordinate interpolation has one component body shared by longitude,
latitude, XY and supplied Z/M. Optional ordinates are interpolated when both
ends carry them. Geographic validation follows explicit axis interpretation;
the general carrier interpolation does not infer a height or datum.

Exact carrier content has one source identity independent of references and
admission. The source-only framing records carrier kind, dimension, collection
and ring structure, and every normalized rational ordinate including Z/M. The
geographic prepared identity prefixes the same framing with its interpolation
and reference laws. Changing spare capacity or equivalent decimal spelling does
not change either source content or mathematical output identity. Capacity and
heap-limb evidence remains in the separate preparation receipt.

Selected curve contacts retain source laws and unrounded line propagation.
Coordinate-linear contacts use the existing exact segment predicate after
longitude translations. Their exact parameters are recovered by the same
source interpolation home. Equal physical source endpoints identify seams and
poles without an angular tolerance. Equal uniquely selected endpoint curves
also prove coincidence under orientation reversal.

For isolated contacts, a complete parameter box maps to unit geodetic-normal
images and their parameter derivatives. A disjoint component enclosure proves
complete physical separation. Otherwise the atlas subdivides until both images
have a shared component with a certified nonzero common sign. Equality of the
other two components then implies equality of the omitted component because
both normals have unit length and the same square-root sign. This supplies a
regular two-coordinate chart at seams and poles without an angular tolerance.

The shared two-variable interval root home proves existence and uniqueness
using a Krawczyk self-map and strict contraction; an iterate or a narrowing
enclosure alone is insufficient. Exact original endpoint equality can instead
supply existence when strict contraction proves uniqueness on a closed boundary.
A matching operation, target reference, epoch and exact source endpoint gives
this same proof for a symbolic transformed endpoint. Merely enclosing zero is
never an exact-root witness.

Overlapping internal parameter panels preserve complete coverage and place
internal division-face roots in a proof neighbourhood. Every expanded panel
must retain its certified common-sign chart. Roots coalesce only when one
complete root enclosure lies in the other root's uniqueness box; both chart
proofs imply the same physical normal equation. Returned parameter and angular
enclosures are separate proof evidence, never replacements for source curves.

Native prepared polygons additionally retain a certified oriented-ring base.
Left intersects the explicitly traversed ring-left sides; Right selects the
closed complement of that complete intersection. Written/Complement then
selects that immutable base without reparsing. Exact selected geodesics and
continuous operation images remain curves, and the optional written chart is
absent for such native regions. Original-law native membership and complete
boundary-box certificates use the smooth geodetic-normal atlas described in
[its mathematical law](../atlas/LAW.md).

## Symbolic point inventories and coordinate admission

An operation-image point remains a dimension-zero component. Its immutable
source, actual operation chain, target reference and epoch determine its native
image identity; no quantized output coordinate enters geographic source content.
Prepared native parts append a framed symbolic-point block only when that
inventory is nonempty, so adding the new representation preserves every existing
empty-inventory identity. Aggregate preparation work and storage receipts remain
separate metadata and cannot remint the mathematical source identity.

Owned and borrowed coordinate factories apply the same original axis/range law.
The borrowed factory admits complete original XY/Z/M limbs before cloning, and
both factories admit exact angular comparisons before evaluating them. Pure,
exact-context and numerical-context source interpolation share one original
ordinate difference/product/add body. Each admitted arithmetic step uses the
actual reduced operand widths through the XSD cost home; missing Z/M is never
invented by interpolation.
