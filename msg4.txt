Route the `purrdf-core` canonical entry points through the labelling engine

The root canonical functions (`try_canonicalize`, `try_canonicalize_view`,
`try_canonical_digest_view`, `try_graph_digest_view`, the checked variants,
`canonical_relabel` and `canonical_relabel_with_mapping`) now run the
nested-refinement labelling and return its canonical document and the digest
of its line sequence as a `Digest32`. The RDFC-1.0 functions stay reachable
only under `rdfc12`, with their `CanonHash` argument.

A composite literal is read as a literal: the labels spelled in its text are
text, relabeling copies it as written, and `datasets_isomorphic` no longer
pre-rejects on the dictionary blank count. A reserved-namespace IRI is
ordinary data. Receipts gain `SearchEvent::LabellingUnit`, and the result
reports no presentation (`None`) for the line-sequence document.

The accounting test is rewritten from the engine's own poll and reservation
counts, the search differential that pinned the old search is removed, and the
identity fixtures that moved by design are updated.
