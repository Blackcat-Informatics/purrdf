### PurRDF Licensing Explanation

This file is an explanatory draft and has not undergone legal review. For the independent model backtranslation record, see
[Backtranslation Check Explanation](./LICENSING-BACKTRANSLATION.md). This file cannot replace the full license texts.

PurRDF first-party code allows users to choose any one of MIT, Apache-2.0, or MulanPSL-2.0.
The SPDX expression is `MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR MulanPSL-2.0`.
These are three open-source licensing options. Commercial licensing is provided separately by the copyright holder and is not a fourth open-source option.
Documents marked `CC-BY-4.0` retain that license.

Article 6 of the [complete Chinese and English texts of MulanPSL-2.0](../LICENSE-MULAN) provides that the Chinese and English versions have equal legal effect;
if they conflict or are inconsistent, the Chinese version governs. Article 2 provides that each contributor grants you a patent license in accordance with its terms.
If you or your affiliated entities directly or indirectly initiate patent-infringement litigation concerning this software or contributions within it (including counterclaims or crossclaims)
or other actions to enforce patent rights, the patent license granted to you for this software by this license terminates as of the date that action is initiated.
The scope and conditions of the patent license are governed by the full text of Article 2. Article 3 grants no trademark license, except for use necessary to fulfill the notice obligations in Article 4.
Article 4 requires that recipients be provided with a copy of the license and that copyright,
patent, trademark, and disclaimer notices be retained. The text in distribution packages must be byte-for-byte identical to the bilingual text whose digest is fixed in the repository.

Third-party code, Unicode data, and inherited materials retain their original licenses. Choosing MulanPSL-2.0 does not
relicense third-party materials. The generated Unicode tables are also subject to `Unicode-3.0`.
The YAML output implementation retains the MIT license notice of the reference implementation; the original licenses of test data also continue to apply.
XML conformance test data are obtained locally and are not included in distribution packages.

Cargo, the two Python distribution packages, npm/WASM, and C distribution packages contain complete licenses, applicable third-party notices,
source inventories, and digests. Release checks verify the bytes in the actual archives and retain the verification result for each artifact.
For material sources and applicable scope, see the [English explanation](../LICENSING.md), source records, and the SPDX identifiers in the files;
the actual grants and obligations are governed by the full text of each license. An explanatory document cannot change the language provision of MulanPSL-2.0.
